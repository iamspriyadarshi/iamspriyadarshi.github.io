I am currently a Pre-Doctoral Research Fellow in [Mphasis AI & Applied Tech Lab at Ashoka University](https://www.ashoka.edu.in/page/mphasis-lab/), working under the supervision of [Prof. Debayan Gupta](https://www.ashoka.edu.in/profile/debayan-gupta/) from [Ashoka University](https://www.ashoka.edu.in), and [Prof. Shubhasis Haldar](https://www.shubhasis-haldar-cmt.com/) from [S.N. Bose National Centre for Basic Sciences](https://www.bose.res.in/). I hold a Bachelor's degree with a major in Biology and a minor in Computer Science, followed by a Postgraduate Diploma in Research with a thesis in Computer Science and a concentration in Chemistry, both from [Ashoka University](https://www.ashoka.edu.in).

My research lies at the intersection of biology and computation, with a strong focus on Computational Oncology. I am particularly passionate about developing data-driven methodologies to enhance cancer prevention, diagnosis, and treatment strategies. Recently, I have also developed a keen interest in AI-powered virtual cell models, which hold immense potential for simulating complex cellular behaviors and understanding disease mechanisms. Through this interdisciplinary work, I aim to drive progress in precision medicine and contribute meaningfully to biomedical innovation.
